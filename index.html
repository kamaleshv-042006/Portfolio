About

Engineer's mindset, analyst's curiosity

I came to software through an interest in how systems actually behave — not just how they look. That has pulled me toward backend engineering: modelling data properly, writing queries that stay fast as the tables grow, and exposing that logic through clean APIs.

My internship at Cube AI Solutions gave me hands-on web development work alongside data handling; at Airpina Solutions I moved deeper into SQL, data cleaning and reporting, where the value of careful preprocessing became obvious. Those two experiences shaped how I build: get the data right first, and the interface follows.

Outside coursework I sharpen my problem solving on LeetCode and keep small projects shipping end to end, because finishing is a skill of its own.
